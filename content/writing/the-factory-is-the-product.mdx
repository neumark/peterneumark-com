Most teams ship an application. A few ship the machine that ships applications. The second
group is quietly winning, and it has little to do with headcount.

When a language model writes most of the code, the scarce skill moves one level up: deciding
what gets built, verifying that it was, and keeping the loop short enough that nobody loses
the thread. That loop is the factory. It has a floor plan, a shift schedule and a quality
gate, even if nobody drew them.

## What a floor plan looks like

Start with the one thing every change passes through. In our case it is a single verify
script. If it does not run there, it did not happen.

<Quote>A build step you cannot see is a build step you cannot fix.</Quote>

The rest of this post walks through the three gates we settled on and what each one caught
in its first month.
